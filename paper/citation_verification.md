Verified on 2026-11-25 using primary publisher/proceedings pages, author-submitted arXiv records and official software/model/dataset documentation. All references are cited in the body. Metadata and claim-relevant abstracts/documentation were read; selected SQuAD evaluation and Koehn bootstrap PDF sections were also inspected. This is not a claim of exhaustive full-text review of every publication.

All entries: existence, author list/corporate author, title, publication year (or explicitly undated), venue/publisher, and official URL/DOI checked. ArXiv-only sources are identified as preprints, not peer-reviewed conference publications. Dynamic documentation is undated unless a revision date is displayed.
